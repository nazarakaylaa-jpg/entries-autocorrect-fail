Entry for the [Funniest Autocorrect Fail](https://make.fun/c/u-zeroxfive-funniest-autocorrect-fail-ddf82154) competition on make.fun.

## The entry

> "I just adopted a god from the shelter and he keeps shedding on the couch and judging me."

One line, 88 characters. The slip is a single-letter autocorrect, dog to god:
the intended word is instantly recoverable ("adopted ... from the shelter"),
the wrong word is surprising and specific, and the line reads exactly like a
real text — a god who sheds on the couch and judges you.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the one-line entry (88 characters, a single sentence) to
`/output/entry.txt`. Run it locally with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
